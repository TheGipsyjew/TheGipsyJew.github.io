# How to export Amazon reviews to CSV or Excel

By TheGipsyjew · Published and checked on 13 November 2026 · [Editorial method](https://thegipsyjew.github.io/reviewpluck/method/)

The short answer

Install the ReviewPluck Chrome extension, sign in to Amazon on the marketplace you need, open the product page or paste its ASIN, start the extraction, then export the file. CSV is available on every plan; XLSX and JSON come with Pro. The free plan keeps the first 10 reviews per extraction, 3 extractions a month.

## What you need before you start

- Google Chrome (or a Chromium browser that installs Chrome Web Store extensions).

- A ReviewPluck account, even on the free plan. Sign-in is by e-mail code or Google.

- An Amazon customer account on the marketplace you extract from (amazon.com, amazon.co.uk, amazon.de…), signed in in the same browser. Amazon shows the full review list to signed-in customers.

Copy-pasting reviews by hand works for a dozen. Past that, an extension that walks through the review pages for you saves hours and keeps one review per row.

## Step 1: install the extension

Open the [ReviewPluck listing on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/reviewpluck/bjaoeenljkcnhdffjmffopimkpglkcme) and click Add to Chrome. Pin it to the toolbar so the popup is one click away.

## Step 2: open the product

Go to the product page on any of the 24 supported Amazon marketplaces, or paste the product’s ASIN into the extension. ReviewPluck takes you to the product’s “All reviews” page.

## Step 3: start the extraction

Before you start, the extension shows a time estimate based on the number of reviews Amazon reports. Once it runs, you can close the popup and switch tabs: the extraction continues in the background.

If Amazon asks for a CAPTCHA or a sign-in, the extraction pauses. Complete the check on Amazon and click Resume: it picks up where it stopped, and the reviews already collected stay exportable.

## Step 4: export the file

Each extraction produces one file, even a partial one. CSV is included on every plan and opens in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or LibreOffice. Pro adds XLSX and JSON, filters (rating, period, verified purchase, images, variant, keyword) to export only a selection, and a history of every run per product.

Free and Pro, as published on 13 November 2026

| Limit | Free | Pro

| Reviews per extraction | First 10 | Unlimited

| Extractions per month | 3 | Unlimited

| Formats | CSV | CSV, XLSX, JSON

| Filters and history | No | Yes

Pro costs €4.99 a month, €47.88 a year or €99.99 once (prices including VAT), with a 7-day free trial on the monthly and yearly plans. Check the [pricing page](https://reviewpluck.com/en/tarifs/) for current terms.

## Why the file can have fewer reviews than Amazon shows

The number displayed on a product page counts ratings, and some reviews are not listed on the marketplace you are browsing. ReviewPluck collects every review the marketplace makes available and always shows how many were collected out of the reported total, so you know what you have.
