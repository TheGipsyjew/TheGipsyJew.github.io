Exporting Amazon reviews: questions and answers

By TheGipsyjew · Published and checked on 13 November 2026 · Editorial method

The short answer

ReviewPluck is a Chrome extension that collects the reviews of an Amazon product and exports them to CSV (all plans) or XLSX and JSON (Pro). It works on 24 Amazon marketplaces, runs in your browser and does not store the reviews on its servers.

Can I export Amazon reviews for free?

Yes. The free plan exports the first 10 reviews of a product per extraction, 3 extractions a month, in CSV. Pro removes both limits and adds XLSX, JSON, filters and history.

Which accounts do I need?

A ReviewPluck account, even for the free plan, with sign-in by e-mail code or Google, and an Amazon customer account signed in on each marketplace you extract from.

Why are there fewer reviews in my file than on Amazon?

The figure shown by Amazon includes ratings without text and reviews the marketplace does not list. ReviewPluck collects every review the marketplace makes available and shows how many it collected out of the reported total.

What happens if Amazon shows a CAPTCHA?

The extraction pauses and what was already collected stays exportable. Complete the check on Amazon, click Resume, and the extraction continues from where it stopped.

Which Amazon marketplaces are supported?

24 marketplaces, including amazon.com, .co.uk, .de, .fr, .it, .es, .nl, .se, .pl, .ca, .com.mx and .co.jp. The extension interface is available in 14 languages.

Are the exported reviews stored by ReviewPluck?

No. The extraction runs in your browser and the history stays there. The ReviewPluck account is only used to check your plan.

Can I put the reviews on my own store?

The CSV contains rating, title, text, author, date, verified purchase, variant, link and photos, which most store import tools can map. Before republishing review text elsewhere, check Amazon’s Conditions of Use and your store platform’s rules on imported reviews.

How do I cancel Pro?

From the Account tab of the extension, at any time. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. The 7-day trial on monthly and yearly plans is free if cancelled before it ends.
